# DRG Tyrant Bingo - Quick Guide

Welcome to the DRG Tyrant Bingo! This is a real-time multiplayer bingo game. Here is a quick tutorial on how everything works.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Join the Game**: When you open the link, you will be prompted to enter a **Pseudo** (your username).
2. **Your Grid**: Once you enter your pseudo, the game will automatically generate a unique 4x4 Bingo grid for you, using the current pool of challenges.
3. **Checking Boxes**: During the game, simply click on a square to mark it as completed. The box will turn blue. 
4. **Real-time**: Your progress is instantly synchronized. The admins can see your score and exactly which boxes you have checked in real-time!
5. **Winning**: If you manage to check all 16 boxes, a "You Won!" screen will pop up.
