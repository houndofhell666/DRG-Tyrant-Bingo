# DRG Tyrant Bingo - Quick Guide

Welcome to the DRG Tyrant Bingo! This is a real-time multiplayer bingo game. Here is a quick tutorial on how everything works, split into the Player side and the Admin side.

## 🎮 How to Play (Player Side)

1. **Join the Game**: When you open the link, you will be prompted to enter a **Pseudo** (your username).
2. **Your Grid**: Once you enter your pseudo, the game will automatically generate a unique 4x4 Bingo grid for you, using the current pool of challenges.
3. **Checking Boxes**: During the game, simply click on a square to mark it as completed. The box will turn blue. 
4. **Real-time**: Your progress is instantly synchronized. The admins can see your score and exactly which boxes you have checked in real-time!
5. **Winning**: If you manage to check all 16 boxes, a "You Won!" screen will pop up.

## ⚙️ How to Manage the Game (Admin Side)

As an admin, you have access to a secret dashboard to monitor players and manage the game.

### Accessing the Dashboard
- On the main game screen, look at the big jewel at the very top of the background image.
- **Click on the top jewel** to open the hidden admin portal.
- Enter the secret password.

### Features of the Dashboard

The admin dashboard has two main tabs:

**1. Leaderboard (Live Scores)**
- You will see a live ranking of all currently active players and their scores (e.g., `10 / 16`).
- **Inspect a Grid**: Click on any player's name or score in the list. A pop-up will appear showing you their exact 4x4 grid and exactly which challenges they have completed. This is perfect for verifying a win!
- **Reset Scores**: The big red "Reset All Scores" button will wipe the leaderboard clean. Use this when starting a brand new round.

**2. Manage Challenges**
- Switch to this tab to see the "pool" of possible challenges that the game uses to generate player grids.
- **Add**: Type a new challenge in the text box at the bottom and click "Add".
- **Delete**: Click the "X" next to any existing challenge to remove it from the pool.
- *Note: Changes here will apply to new players when they generate their grid. Existing players will keep their current grids until the round ends.*
